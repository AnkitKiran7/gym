Exercise Guide

22M · 162cm · 59kg  ·  Monday / Wednesday / Friday  ·  Full Gym

Beginner → Intermediate

Monday

Push — Chest · Shoulders · Triceps + Leg Accessory
Bench Press
Chest · Triceps · Front Delts
4 × 8–10
Jeff Nippard — Bench Press
Key cue
Retract shoulder blades before every rep. Chest up, shoulders back and down.
Bar path
Touch lower chest. Elbows at 45–60°, never flared wide.
❌ Avoid
Bouncing bar off chest · Hips lifting off bench · Elbows flaring 90° out
💡 Spine tip: Slight natural arch in lower back is fine and protective. Squeeze shoulder blades like cracking a walnut. This is the #1 form cue for shoulder health.
Breathing
Inhale ↓ on descent — Exhale ↑ on press
Overhead Press
Shoulders · Triceps · Upper Traps
3 × 8–10
Jeff Nippard — Overhead Press
Core
Brace abs + squeeze glutes the entire set. Zero lower back arch.
Head
Tuck chin as bar passes face. Push head through at lockout.
❌ Avoid
Leaning back excessively · Not locking out at top · Bar path drifting forward
💡 Spine tip: OHP is a full spinal alignment test every rep. Neutral spine, braced core. Strengthens deep stabilizing muscles around your thoracic spine.
Breathing
Big inhale before press — Exhale at lockout
Incline DB Press
Upper Chest · Front Delts
3 × 10–12
Incline Dumbbell Press — Form Guide
Angle
Set bench to 30–45°. Higher angle = shoulder press, not chest.
Range
Full stretch at bottom, hard squeeze at top. No half reps.
❌ Avoid
Incline too steep · Short range of motion · Uneven dumbbell height
💡 Spine tip: Upper back stays fully in contact with bench. Scapulae retracted and depressed — same as flat bench press.
Breathing
Inhale ↓ descent — Exhale ↑ press
Lateral Raises
Side Delts
3 × 12–15
Lateral Raises — Proper Form
Height
Raise to shoulder height ONLY. Above that = traps, not side delts.
Tilt
Tilt DBs slightly — little finger higher than thumb. Like pouring water.
❌ Avoid
Heavy weight + swinging · Raising above shoulders · Shrugging traps
💡 Spine tip: Stand perfectly upright — neutral spine, slight knee bend. Lateral raises done with control reinforce upright posture under load.
Breathing
Exhale ↑ raise — Inhale ↓ lower
Tricep Pushdown
Triceps
3 × 12
Cable Tricep Pushdown — Form Guide
Key cue
Elbows pinned to your ribs. Only forearms move — nothing else.
Lockout
Fully extend arms. Squeeze triceps hard for 1 second at bottom.
❌ Avoid
Elbows drifting out from sides · Using bodyweight to push · No lockout
💡 Spine tip: Slight forward hinge at hips keeps torso stable without loading the lower back. Core stays braced.
Breathing
Exhale pushing down — Inhale returning up
Diamond Push-ups
Triceps · Chest (Calisthenics Finisher)
2 × max
Diamond Push-ups — Proper Form
Hands
Index fingers + thumbs form a diamond under your chest.
Body
Rigid plank — head to heel. Zero hip sag or raised backside.
❌ Avoid
Hips sagging · Elbows flaring wide · Partial range of motion
💡 Spine tip: Best bodyweight spinal alignment drill. Brace core before you start. This is also your calisthenics strength benchmark — track your max reps weekly.
Breathing
Inhale ↓ down — Exhale ↑ push up
Leg Press
Quads · Glutes (Monday Accessory)
3 × 10
Leg Press — Form & Mistakes
Feet
Shoulder-width on mid-platform. Toes pointed slightly out.
Depth
90° knee angle max. Deeper = lower back rounds off the pad.
❌ Avoid
Lower back peeling off pad · Knees caving in · Locking knees at top
💡 Spine tip: Full back contact with the pad at all times. If your lower back rounds at the bottom, reduce depth or reduce weight immediately.
Breathing
Inhale ↓ lower — Exhale ↑ press
Leg Curl
Hamstrings (Monday Accessory)
3 × 12
Lying Leg Curl — Proper Form
Key cue
Press hips into pad throughout. Curl heels toward glutes fully.
Tempo
2 sec curl up, 2–3 sec slow lower. Slow negatives = muscle growth.
❌ Avoid
Hips rising off pad · Swinging with momentum · Short range of motion
💡 Spine tip: Prone position naturally decompresses the lumbar spine. Keep pelvis neutral — don't tuck or hyperextend throughout the set.
Breathing
Exhale ↑ curl up — Inhale ↓ lower

Wednesday

Legs + Core — Heavy Day
Barbell Squat
Quads · Glutes · Hamstrings
4 × 8–10
Jeff Nippard — How To Squat
Depth
Hip crease BELOW the knees. Anything above is a partial squat.
Knees
Push knees out in line with toes. Never let them cave inward (valgus).
❌ Avoid
Knees caving in · Heels lifting off floor · Good morning squat (torso folds forward)
💡 Spine tip: Valsalva maneuver — big breath in, brace 360° like someone will punch your stomach. This is your natural spinal protection belt under heavy load. Never skip this.
Breathing
Big breath + brace before descending — Exhale at top
Romanian Deadlift
Hamstrings · Glutes · Lower Back
3 × 10
Romanian Deadlift — Complete Guide
Hip hinge
Push hips BACK — not down. Feel the hamstring stretch. This is not a squat.
Bar path
Bar stays touching your legs the whole way — drags from thighs to shins.
❌ Avoid
Rounding lower back · Bending knees too much · Bar drifting away from body
💡 Spine tip: The RDL teaches neutral spine under load better than almost any exercise. Master this and your posture in everyday life improves dramatically. Think "long spine from tailbone to crown."
Breathing
Inhale + brace before hinging — Exhale driving hips forward
Leg Extension
Quads
3 × 12
Leg Extension — Proper Form
Setup
Knee joint must align with machine pivot. Adjust seat before starting.
Squeeze
Fully extend and hold — squeeze quad hard for 1 second at top.
❌ Avoid
Seat too far from pivot · Not fully extending · Using momentum to swing
💡 Spine tip: Sit upright, lower back pressed into pad. No leaning forward — that shifts stress away from quads and onto your spine.
Breathing
Exhale extending — Inhale lowering
Leg Curl
Hamstrings
3 × 12
Lying Leg Curl — Proper Form
Key cue
Hips pressed into pad the whole set. Curl heels to glutes fully.
Tempo
2 sec up, 2–3 sec slow lower. Controlled negative = most growth.
❌ Avoid
Hips rising off pad · Swinging with momentum · Stopping short of full range
💡 Spine tip: Prone decompresses the lumbar. Neutral pelvis throughout — don't tuck or hyperextend.
Breathing
Exhale ↑ curl up — Inhale ↓ lower
Calf Raises
Gastrocnemius · Soleus
4 × 15
Calf Raises — Form & Range of Motion
Range
Full stretch at bottom, full raise at top. Pause 1 second at peak.
Platform
Use a raised step with heels hanging off for maximum range.
❌ Avoid
Bouncing at the bottom · No pause at top · Half range of motion
💡 Spine tip: Stand perfectly upright. Calf raises reinforce ankle-to-spine alignment — good posture here carries into how you walk and run every single day.
Breathing
Exhale ↑ rising — Inhale ↓ lowering
Hanging Knee Raises
Core · Abs · Hip Flexors · Grip
3 × 15
Hanging Knee Raises — Proper Form
Key cue
At top, tuck pelvis under (posterior tilt) — this is what activates your abs.
Control
Lower legs slowly. No swinging or kipping — control is everything.
❌ Avoid
Swinging with momentum · No pelvic tilt at top · Shoulders shrugging up
💡 Spine tip: After your sets, do an extra 30-sec dead hang. It decompresses your entire spine — undoes hours of sitting. Also builds grip strength for pull-ups.
Breathing
Exhale ↑ raising knees — Inhale ↓ lowering
Plank
Full Core · Transverse Abdominis · Spinal Stabilizers
3 × 45 sec
Perfect Plank Form — Jeff Cavaliere
Body line
Perfect straight line head to heel. No sagging hips, no raised backside.
Core cue
Brace like someone's about to punch you. Squeeze glutes simultaneously.
❌ Avoid
Hips dropping or piking up · Holding breath · Head drooping forward
💡 Spine tip: The plank trains your transverse abdominis — your body's natural back belt. Master this and every other exercise becomes safer. It is the foundation of all spinal stability.
Breathing
Slow controlled breathing throughout — never hold your breath during a plank

Friday

Pull — Back · Biceps + Leg Accessory
Pull-ups
Lats · Biceps · Rear Delts (Calisthenics)
4 × max
Jeff Nippard — How To Do Pull-ups
Initiate
Depress + retract scapulae FIRST, then drive elbows down to ribs. Don't just arm-pull.
Full hang
Dead hang every rep — arms fully extended at bottom. Non-negotiable.
❌ Avoid
Kipping / swinging · Not reaching full hang · Shoulders shrugging up to ears
💡 Spine tip: After your sets, hang for 30 extra seconds. The dead hang decompresses your entire spine and undoes hours of sitting. Biggest free gift you can give your back.
Breathing
Exhale ↑ pulling up — Inhale ↓ lowering
Seated Cable Row
Mid Back · Lats · Biceps
3 × 10–12
Seated Cable Row — Proper Form
Pull target
Pull to lower chest/upper abdomen. Elbows pass behind your torso.
Squeeze
Squeeze shoulder blades hard at peak — hold for 1 second every rep.
❌ Avoid
Rounding back on return · Using torso momentum · No shoulder blade squeeze
💡 Spine tip: Your #1 posture-correcting exercise. Squeezing shoulder blades together directly counteracts the forward-rounded posture from hours of phone and desk use. Prioritise the squeeze above all.
Breathing
Exhale pulling — Inhale extending arms back
Lat Pulldown
Lats · Teres Major · Biceps
3 × 10–12
Lat Pulldown — Jeff Nippard
Initiate
Shoulders down first (depress scapulae), then drive elbows toward back pockets.
Target
Bar to upper chest / clavicle. Never behind the neck — serious injury risk.
❌ Avoid
Pulling behind the neck · Leaning too far back · Pulling with biceps not lats
💡 Spine tip: Think "elbows to back pockets" — this cue externally rotates your shoulder and protects the rotator cuff from impingement every single rep.
Breathing
Exhale ↓ pulling down — Inhale ↑ returning
DB Bent-over Row
Lats · Mid Back · Rear Delts
3 × 10
Dumbbell Row — Proper Form
Hinge
Hip hinge to 45–60° from vertical. Back flat — not rounded, not hyperextended.
Row path
Pull elbow past your hip — "elbow to back pocket." Feel the lat fully.
❌ Avoid
Rounding lower back · Not hinging enough · Short range of motion
💡 Spine tip: Teaches the hip hinge under load — critical for a healthy lower back in daily life. Mastering flat-back hinge means everything you pick off the floor is done safely.
Breathing
Exhale ↑ rowing up — Inhale ↓ lowering
Face Pulls
Rear Delts · Rotator Cuff · Posture
3 × 15
Face Pulls — Jeff Cavaliere (Athlean-X)
Cable height
Set cable at forehead height. Pull rope to face, splitting at your ears.
External rotation
At peak, rotate so hands go behind your ears. This is the whole exercise.
❌ Avoid
Elbows dropping below shoulder height · Going too heavy · No external rotation at peak
💡 Spine tip: The most underrated posture exercise in any gym. Directly strengthens the muscles that pull your shoulders back. Light weight, high reps, full rotation — every session without fail.
Breathing
Exhale pulling to face — Inhale extending out
Barbell / DB Curl
Biceps · Brachialis
3 × 12
Bicep Curl — Jeff Nippard
Elbows
Elbows stay pinned to your sides. They must not travel forward when curling.
Supinate
With DBs — rotate palms outward as you curl for full bicep contraction.
❌ Avoid
Swinging torso to lift · Elbows drifting forward · Dropping weight on way down
💡 Spine tip: Stand upright — neutral spine, core braced. Swinging curls put repeated stress on lumbar vertebrae over hundreds of reps. Never worth the ego lift.
Breathing
Exhale ↑ curling — Inhale ↓ 3-sec slow lower
Bulgarian Split Squat
Quads · Glutes · Hip Flexors (Friday Accessory)
3 × 10 each
Bulgarian Split Squat — Full Guide
Front foot
Far enough forward that front shin is vertical at the bottom of the squat.
Rear foot
Laces on bench (not heel). Rear knee hovers just above floor at the bottom.
❌ Avoid
Front foot too close (knee shoots over toes) · Torso collapsing forward · Slamming rear knee
💡 Spine tip: If your torso falls forward, your hip flexors are tight — stretch them daily. Tight hip flexors are one of the biggest causes of lower back pain. This exercise reveals it and fixes it over time.
Breathing
Inhale ↓ descending — Exhale ↑ driving up
